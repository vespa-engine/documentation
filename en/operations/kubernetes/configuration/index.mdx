These pages cover configuring a Vespa on Kubernetes deployment.

- [Configure Local Storage Type](/en/operations/kubernetes/configuration/configure-local-storage-type) — provision node-local storage for content clusters to maximize performance.
- [Configure Log Collections](/en/operations/kubernetes/configuration/logging) — collect and forward Vespa logs with Fluent Bit and Loki.
- [Configure External Access Layer](/en/operations/kubernetes/configuration/ingress) — expose Vespa endpoints outside the Kubernetes cluster.
- [Configure Custom Overrides](/en/operations/kubernetes/configuration/custom-overrides-podtemplate) — customize Vespa pods with `PodTemplateSpec` overlays.
- [Configure TLS Encryption with CertManager](/en/operations/kubernetes/configuration/tls) — enable mTLS for internal communication and TLS for ingress traffic.
- [Configure Monitoring](/en/operations/kubernetes/configuration/monitoring) — collect metrics with the Prometheus Operator and forward them to Grafana Cloud.
