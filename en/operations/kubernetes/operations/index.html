These pages cover deploying and operating Vespa on Kubernetes, from installing the Vespa Operator to day-to-day operations.

  • Installation — deploy the Vespa Operator using the official Helm chart and create your first VespaSet.
  • Permissions — the RBAC permissions required by the Vespa Operator and the ConfigServers.
  • Pod Lifecycle — automatic recovery, graceful shutdown, and disruption handling.
  • Production Serving — harden a deployment for production: node placement and data redundancy.
  • Vespa Upgrades — perform rolling upgrades of the Vespa version.
  • Resource Scaling — scale the resources of a Vespa deployment.
