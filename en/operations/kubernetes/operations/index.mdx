These pages cover deploying and operating Vespa on Kubernetes, from installing the Vespa Operator to day-to-day operations.

- [Installation](/en/operations/kubernetes/operations/installation) — deploy the Vespa Operator using the official Helm chart and create your first `VespaSet`.
- [Permissions](/en/operations/kubernetes/operations/permissions) — the RBAC permissions required by the Vespa Operator and the ConfigServers.
- [Pod Lifecycle](/en/operations/kubernetes/operations/pod-lifecycle) — automatic recovery, graceful shutdown, and disruption handling.
- [Production Serving](/en/operations/kubernetes/operations/production-serving) — harden a deployment for production: node placement and data redundancy.
- [Vespa Upgrades](/en/operations/kubernetes/operations/upgrades) — perform rolling upgrades of the Vespa version.
- [Resource Scaling](/en/operations/kubernetes/operations/resource-scaling) — scale the resources of a Vespa deployment.
- [Delete a VespaSet](/en/operations/kubernetes/operations/delete-vespaset) — tear down a Vespa application or an entire deployment.
